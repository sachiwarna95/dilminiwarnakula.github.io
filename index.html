Modeling how heart cells beat, and how drugs change that.

I’m a PhD student in Mathematics and Statistics at Georgia State University. I build mathematical models of mouse ventricular myocytes, the muscle cells of the heart, to study their electrical activity and contraction.

My work focuses on hypertrophic and failing myocytes, early afterdepolarizations (EADs), and the effects of β-adrenergic stimulation. I work with Dr. Vladimir Bondarenko, and I’m a Molecular Basis of Disease (MBD) Doctoral Fellow.

I trained as a biomedical engineer at the University of Moratuwa in Sri Lanka, where I later taught mathematics before starting my PhD.
